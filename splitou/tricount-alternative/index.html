Why Look for an Alternative to Tricount?

Tricount is a widely used and reliable tool for running group balances. However, it was conceived for manual data entry. In the most common real-world scenario — "I paid the restaurant bill for eight people, who owes what?" — Tricount leaves you doing manual math: you have to type the total, decipher the receipt, and divide the amounts by hand.

Key pain points driving users towards Splitou:

  • No line-item receipt breakdown: Tricount lets you attach a receipt photo, but doesn't read the individual dishes or prices.
  • Tedious restaurant splitting: The person who paid ends up doing all the accounting work.
  • Modern European payment shortcuts: Splitou embeds direct actions for Revolut, PayPal, Wero, and Lydia.
  • Bank-independent tool: Tricount was acquired by the Dutch neobank bunq in 2022 to promote banking cards. Splitou remains 100% focused on user privacy.

What Makes Splitou Unique

AI Receipt Parsing in Seconds

Snap the bill (up to 3 photos for long receipts). Google Vertex AI Gemini reads items, quantities, prices, and discounts, automatically verifying that the sum matches the total paid.

Zero-Install Guest Experience

Show your QR code: friends point their phone camera and an instant webapp opens in their browser (available in 7 languages). They tick their drinks and dishes in real time. Shared items (like wine) can be split evenly, and no item gets paid twice.

One-Tap Repayment

Friends see the exact amount owed alongside your preferred payment details (Revolut, PayPal, Wero, Lydia, IBAN, cash). Splitou never touches your money directly.

Full Trips & Groups Management

Add expenses by AI scan or manually, choose among 4 flexible split modes, convert foreign currencies, and let the smart debt algorithm compute the minimum transfers to settle up.

16 Lifetime Trophies & Insights

Detailed charts (category breakdown, paid vs consumed, daily spending trend) and 16 permanent awards (Group Banker, Foodie, Happy-Hour King...) saved for life in your profile.

Strict EU Privacy & GDPR

Receipt photos are permanently deleted after AI parsing, shared bills expire after 90 days, and data is hosted in the EU (Google Cloud europe-west1).

When Tricount Might Still Suit You Better

To be completely honest, Tricount remains a solid choice if:

  • You want an app that is 100% free with zero ads and don't need automated receipt scanning;
  • Your travel companions refuse to install any mobile app for ongoing group trips;
  • You are a bunq bank customer looking to auto-sync transactions from your card.

However, for restaurant meals, bar tabs, and everyday bill splitting, Splitou eliminates manual calculation entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best alternative to Tricount for restaurant bills?

Splitou is specifically built for restaurant and outing scenarios where Tricount falls short: AI scans the bill item by item, friends scan a QR code and tick what they had live on a web page without installing an app or registering, then pay back via Revolut, PayPal, Wero, Lydia, or cash.

Is Splitou free like Tricount?

Yes, Splitou is free to use: the free tier includes a monthly quota of AI scans, unlimited QR code bill sharing, and full Trips & Groups mode, supported by unobtrusive ads never shown to guests.

Can Splitou replace Tricount for group holidays?

Yes. Splitou's Trips & Groups mode handles ongoing group accounts: invite friends via link or QR code, add expenses with AI scan or manually, choose among 4 flexible split modes, track multi-currency budgets, and compute the fewest debt settlement transfers.

Do friends need to download Splitou to pay me back?

No for restaurant bills: they scan the host's QR code, an interactive web page opens in their browser (7 languages), they tick their dishes, and see the exact repayment amount. Zero app installation and zero account creation required.
